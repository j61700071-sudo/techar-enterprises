Solar Subsidy for Residential Users

Under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, homeowners can avail up to 40% subsidy on solar installations up to 3 kW and 20% subsidy for 4-10 kW systems.
Eligibility Criteria
Must be a residential homeowner
System must be installed by an approved solar vendor
Net metering should be enabled for grid connectivity

Solar Subsidy for Commercial & Industrial Users

Industries, businesses, and institutions can benefit from tax incentives, accelerated depreciation (up to 40%), and reduced electricity costs through government solar schemes.
Popular Schemes & Incentives
MNRE (Ministry of New & Renewable Energy) Subsidy
State-Specific Solar Incentives
Net Metering Policy for Excess Power Compensation
